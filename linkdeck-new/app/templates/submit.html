What LinkDeck is, in plain terms

Affiliate and referral programs pay you when someone signs up or buys through your link. The hard part is getting people to see it. LinkDeck is a shared place for those links: visitors find deals, and link owners get seen. Each visit shows a different random hand, so every link gets a turn.
