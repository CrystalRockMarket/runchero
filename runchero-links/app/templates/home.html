How it works

  1. Paste an Amazon link. Product page, search results, or a short link.
  2. We clean it up. Tracking codes are removed and the product ID is checked.
  3. The community tag is added. That is how qualifying purchases are credited.
  4. Copy and share. The link goes straight to Amazon. Nothing is redirected or hidden.
