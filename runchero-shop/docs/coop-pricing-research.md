## Meal benchmarks and portion caveat

- Mountain House current official pouch listings commonly show **$12.49 per
  pouch**: <https://mountainhouse.com/collections/pouches>.
- Mountain House Rice & Chicken is currently listed at $12.49 per pouch and
  declares two servings per container on its nutrition panel:
  <https://mountainhouse.com/products/rice-chicken-pouch>.
- Backpacker's Pantry listings span roughly **$7.99–$12.99 per package** for
  examples including mashed potatoes, Pad Thai, risotto, and lasagna:
  <https://backpackerspantry.com/collections/backpacking>.
- Mountain House #10 cans are a different format and are not compared to a
  single hiking pouch; official examples include Beef Stew at $68.99 and Rice
  & Chicken at $54.49, with 9–10 servings listed:
  <https://mountainhouse.com/collections/entrees>.

Seed meal comparisons are **per pouch**, not per serving. Competitor declared
servings vary, and the co-op's final recipes, dried weights, calories, and
portion sizes have not yet been supplied. Do not advertise a per-serving
saving until those are standardized and compared on the same basis. For 50-
and 100-pouch lots, the seeded comparison multiplies the $12.49 competitor
pouch price by package count; it is a package-level benchmark, not a claim
that the products have identical recipes or servings.

## Shelf-life and labeling

Runchero Freeze-Dried is intended to offer long-life sealed meals, with the
producer describing a 20+ year shelf life. Final item pages should state the
validated shelf life for that product and packaging, along with storage
conditions, ingredients, allergens, net dry weight, servings, preparation
directions, and nutrition information from the producer's actual labels.
