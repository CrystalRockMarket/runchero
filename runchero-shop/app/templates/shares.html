How the whole-cow drop works

A whole cow is too much for one household and too big for one sale: federal retail rules cap a single sale at half a carcass. So the whole-cow tier is a coordinated drop. Four households each take a quarter, each with their own order, their own cut sheet, and their own packing choices.

  1. Reserve your quarter with the deposit.
  2. We match you with three neighbors on the same drop date.
  3. You each get your own cut sheet to fill out.
  4. The cow is processed at the USDA plant and delivered together.
