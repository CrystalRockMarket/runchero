Check the unit, not just the number. US and UK gallons, pints and cups are different. Recipes usually say which they mean; when they do not, assume the local measure.

Write down the local ones. A feed bag weight, a bucket volume or a trailer load is more useful than a generic table. Add it to the margin.

Growing degree days

GDD = ((daily max + daily min) / 2) - base temperature. Use the crop base: wheat 0 C, maize 10 C, potatoes 7 C, most insects 10 C. Cap the maximum at the crop's upper limit if your local method says so.
