SUBMIT A BOOK TO THE ARCHIVE

Submissions are switched off in this build. This build is read-only: nothing can be written to the archive, and no files can be uploaded. The form below is the real layout - it is only the wiring that is missing. Turn on the admin phase and this page starts accepting books.

When submissions open, a dropped file is copied into {{ '/home/barn/archive' }}, fingerprinted with a SHA-256 checksum, given a generated pixel cover, and queued for review. Nothing is published without a human saying yes.

SUBMISSION FORM
Public domain text, a scan you own, or a link to where it lives.

WHAT WE ACCEPT
  • Public domain farm books and manuals, in any language.
  • Reference works for livestock, crops, machinery and soil.
  • Local handbooks and almanacs written for this farm.
  • Anything with clear rights, or openly licensed.
WHAT WE DO NOT ACCEPT
  • Copyrighted works with no licence.
  • Scanned magazines or newspapers in bulk.
  • Anything that is really an advert in a coat.
